# Getting help

- **Something broke?** Open a [bug report](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Paste **Help → Copy Diagnostics** (versions and counts only) and avoid pasting prompts or transcripts.
- **Idea or missing feature?** Open a [feature request](../../issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Security problem?** Do not open a public issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Questions about using Claude Code itself** belong with Anthropic's [documentation](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/claude-code/overview).

Pitwall is maintained by volunteers; responses are best-effort.
